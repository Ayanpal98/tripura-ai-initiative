Executive Summary & Context

Bridging Traditional Education to AI-Driven Employability

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly transforming education, employment, entrepreneurship and the workplace. Students, job seekers, and MSMEs need practical capabilities to thrive in the emerging digital economy.

The Bridge

Connecting traditional education to digital skills, AI literacy, employability, and sustainable entrepreneurship across Tripura.

Our Core Vision

To build an AI-aware, digitally empowered, and employment-ready generation capable of adapting to rapidly changing career landscapes.

Core Pillars

5 Pillars of the Training Programme

Designed not as isolated lectures, but as an immersive empowerment curriculum covering technical mastery and responsible usage.

Component 01

AI Literacy & Fundamentals

Demystifying Artificial Intelligence for learners across Tripura, taking participants from basic awareness to hands-on generative AI usage.

Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence & Generative AI
Real-world applications, capabilities and technical limitations
Practical exploration of generative tools for everyday learning
Responsible AI awareness from day one
Component 02

Digital Productivity

Empowering participants with modern technological applications to drastically improve everyday efficiency and output quality.

Advanced Documentation & AI-assisted research
Professional presentations and communication tools
Digital content creation and basic data handling
Streamlining everyday workflow tasks digitally
Component 03

AI for Careers & Employability

Equipping job seekers and students with modern tools to navigate the job market with confidence and high professional readiness.

AI-assisted career research and skill-gap identification
Professional resume optimization and tailoring
Mock interview preparation and communication practice
Workplace digital professional standards
Component 04

AI for Entrepreneurship & MSMEs

Helping local business owners, artisans, and aspiring entrepreneurs scale operations using cost-effective AI solutions.

Business ideation and regional market research
Digital marketing and social media content strategies
Customer communication support and basic automation
AI-enabled business productivity models
Component 05

Responsible & Ethical AI

Instilling vital digital hygiene, cyber safety awareness, and ethical responsibility when interacting with synthetic media and AI tools.

Data privacy, cyber safety, and misinformation awareness
Recognizing deepfakes, AI hallucinations, and copyright issues
Importance of human verification and oversight
Safe and ethical digital citizenship

Practical Delivery

Structured 6-Module Workshop Model

Each 2 to 4-hour workshop integrates live demonstrations, interactive practice, and rigorous pre/post learning assessments.

Module 01

AI Fundamentals

Foundational Understanding

Introduction to what AI is, where it is applied globally, understanding Generative AI, and real-world everyday applications.

Module 02

Practical AI Demonstrations

Live Interactive Sessions

Live walkthroughs involving intelligent research, professional writing, learning assistance, presentations, and content creation.

Module 03

AI for Careers

Employment Readiness

Targeted sessions on career path research, professional resume improvement, interview preparation, and identifying skill gaps.

Module 04

AI for Business & MSMEs

Entrepreneurial Value

Practical guidance on business ideas, digital marketing, customer support automation, and local market research.

Module 05

Responsible AI & Ethics

Safety & Governance

Education on privacy, misinformation detection, AI hallucinations, deepfakes, copyright awareness, and human verification.

Module 06

Structured Assessment

Measurable Outcomes

Rigorous evaluation loop: Pre-Assessment → Training Session → Post-Assessment to accurately compute learning improvement.
